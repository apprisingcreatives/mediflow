# MediFlow Future Features Roadmap

## Phase 1: Revenue Protection (Critical)

### Smart Reminder System
- SMS reminders (critical for PH market)
- WhatsApp/Viber fallback channels
- Multi-step reminders: 24 hours before, 2 hours before
- "Confirm / Cancel" action buttons in messages
- Conversion-focused messaging (not just notifications)

### Auto Rebooking Engine
- When patient cancels, suggest next available slot instantly
- Send rebooking link via SMS/email
- Track rebooking conversion rate

### Waitlist System
- When someone cancels, notify waitlisted patients
- Auto-fill empty slots from waitlist queue
- Priority ordering (first-come or urgency-based)

### Basic Analytics
- No-show rate (%)
- Revenue lost from no-shows
- Peak booking hours

---

## Phase 2: Unlock Premium Pricing

### Online Payments (GCash / Maya)
- Prepaid appointments to increase commitment
- Deposit system (require PHP 100-300 booking deposit, deduct from consultation)
- Payment confirmation flow
- Refund handling for cancellations

### Business Intelligence Dashboard
- Doctor utilization rate
- Patient return rate
- Revenue per doctor
- Cancellation rates per doctor
- Monthly trend reports
- "You lost PHP X this month due to no-shows" insight cards

### Multi-Doctor Stability
- Reliable scheduling across multiple practitioners
- Conflict detection and resolution
- Doctor availability management

---

## Phase 3: Scale

### Multi-Branch Management
- Branch A / Branch B separate dashboards
- Central admin control across all branches
- Branch-level analytics and reporting

### Staff Roles & Permissions
- Receptionist login (limited access)
- Admin vs Doctor permission levels
- Role-based dashboard views
- Audit trail for staff actions

### Advanced Analytics
- Patient demographics breakdown
- Service popularity trends
- Revenue forecasting
- Comparative branch performance

---

## Professional SaaS Expectations

These are silent deal breakers that must be addressed:

- **Custom domain** — clinicname.com/book
- **White-label branding** — clinic logo, colors, no MediFlow branding
- **Mobile-first UX** — most patients book via phone
- **Reliable uptime** — zero bugs during booking flow

---

## Pricing Justification

**Sell the outcome, not the features:**

> "We reduce your no-shows and recover PHP 20K+/month"

The system must do at least one of these strongly:
1. **Increase bookings**
2. **Reduce no-shows**
3. **Save staff time**

Ideally all three — that's when premium pricing becomes easy to sell.
